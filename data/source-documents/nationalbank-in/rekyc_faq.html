Frequently Asked Questions: Periodic Re-KYC Verification

What is the deadline for submitting my Re-KYC documents?

Customers receive automated alerts via SMS and email 60 days prior to their KYC expiry. Non-compliance after repeated reminders may result in partial freeze where credits are allowed but debits are temporarily suspended per RBI operational guidelines.

Can I complete Re-KYC online without visiting a branch?

Yes, individuals whose address and identity details remain unchanged may submit a self-declaration through net banking, mobile app, or registered email. Where changes have occurred, e-KYC authentication or Video KYC can be completed remotely using an Aadhaar registered mobile number.
