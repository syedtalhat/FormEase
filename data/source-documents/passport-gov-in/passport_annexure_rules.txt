MINISTRY OF EXTERNAL AFFAIRS - PASSPORT SEVA SYSTEM
DOCUMENT ADVISORY & APPLICATION REQUIREMENTS

1. PROOF OF DATE OF BIRTH
Applicants born on or after 26/01/1989 must submit a Birth Certificate issued by the Municipal Authority or Registrar of Births and Deaths. School leaving certificates or matriculation pass certificates containing the date of birth are accepted for persons born before that date.

2. PROOF OF PRESENT RESIDENTIAL ADDRESS
Acceptable address documents include recent electricity or water utility bills (not older than 3 months), running bank account passbook with photo and seal, Aadhaar card, or registered rent agreement with a minimum duration of one year.

3. NON-ECR (EMIGRATION CHECK NOT REQUIRED) STATUS
To obtain Non-ECR status on Indian passports, applicants must submit documentary evidence of educational qualifications of class 10 (matriculation) or higher, or proof of assessment of income tax payment for the previous financial year.
