Revenue Department: Family Income Certificate Standards

An income certificate serves as legal proof of the total gross income of an applicant and their parents or guardians from all legal occupations including agriculture, salaried employment, and business.

Issuing Authorities

In Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, Karnataka, and other union territories, certificates are issued online through e-Seva / Aaple Sarkar centers and signed with a verifiable digital signature by the Taluka Executive Magistrate, Naib Tehsildar, or Revenue Officer.

Validity Period

Income certificates are typically valid for the ongoing financial year (April 1 to March 31). Expired certificates from preceding financial years will lead to immediate rejection during institutional scrutiny.
